# Stabilizer AME and local-unitary rigidity

The public boundary for the AME–LU formalization consists of
`RelativeConicArcs.Gates.AMELUAggregate` and its terminal audit
`RelativeConicArcs.Gates.AMELUAggregateAxioms`.

The library develops the finite-dimensional linear algebra, stabilizer and
MDS–CSS dictionaries, marginal and Weyl-coordinate arguments, generic
local-unitary rigidity, product-unitary automorphism groups, extension
splitting, pencil classification, and diagonal-isoduality mechanisms used by
the accompanying manuscript.

The exact 68-module source and target closures are content-addressed in
`trust/source-manifests/ame_lu.json` and `trust/manifests/ame_lu.json`.
Every printed terminal is kernel checked and uses no project-local axiom.

Hilbert-symmetry, and hyperplane-square gates:

- `RelativeConicArcs.Gates.ClebschArithmeticGluing`;
- `RelativeConicArcs.Gates.ClebschHilbertSymmetry`;
- `RelativeConicArcs.Gates.ClebschHyperplaneSquare`.

Together they check the rank-three split/fused arithmetic, frozen matching
and transporter data, the Hilbert-function forcing step, and the
hyperplane-square annihilator mechanism. The exact 37-module source and
target closures are recorded in
`trust/source-manifests/clebsch_factorization.json` and
`trust/manifests/clebsch_factorization.json`. No project-local axiom is used.
